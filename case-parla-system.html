How I built an AI sales assistant for a Facebook page

Watch on YouTube

The problem

An Italian course business needed a full growth system around the teaching: a brand, content people want to watch, paid ads that bring the right students, and sales chats that actually close.

Constraints

  • Small ad budget.
  • Sales happen in chat, mostly on WhatsApp and Messenger, where tracking is weak.
  • Prices and promises must be exactly right in every reply.

How it works

  1. Content strategy and publishing calendar
  2. Creative assets and videos
  3. Rebranded Facebook page, new Instagram page
  4. Meta ads
  5. Sales chats
  6. Sales assistant: scripted answers first, Gemini 3.1 fallback (Groq as backup), human handoff
  7. A1 and A2 books

Decisions and why

Own the whole funnel, not just the ads

Ads only work if the content, the page they land on and the reply in the chat all hold up, so I planned and produced all of it.

Audit the chats before automating them

Reading more than 100 sales chats showed slow first replies and prices that were never sent. A bot that answers badly faster would not fix that, so a reply playbook and a lead tracker came first.

Scripted answers first, AI second

Predictable questions are answered by scripted rules, with more than 330 automated tests checking them. Only questions they don't cover go to Gemini 3.1 (Groq if Gemini is unavailable), which keeps token use down and the common answers exactly right.

Price guardrails and a human handoff

The assistant must never invent a price or a promise. Anything outside what it is allowed to say goes to a person.

What broke, and the fix

Problem: Production hosting failed after Meta approved the app.

Fix: Fixed. The assistant is hosted and answers customers on Messenger.

Results

  • Content strategy, creative assets, videos, rebranded social pages and Meta campaigns, all planned and produced by me.
  • Redesigned A1 and A2 books, with a QR-linked audio companion.
  • More than 100 sales chats audited. In a detailed sample of 31, the median first reply was 2 hours 20 minutes and 15 of 26 ad-driven chats never received a price.
  • A 25-scenario reply playbook and a lead tracker (New, Qualified, Paid, Lost with reason).
  • In the audited ad period, WhatsApp-button ads cost about 2.50 EGP per chat against 9.63 EGP on Messenger.
  • The sales assistant now answers every chat immediately: first reply in seconds instead of hours, so leads aren't lost while they wait, and no extra person is hired to answer chats by hand.

Limits

  • Campaign figures describe the audited period. They are diagnostic and claim no sales lift.
  • Questions outside the tested scenarios and the AI fallback's limits still go to a person.
